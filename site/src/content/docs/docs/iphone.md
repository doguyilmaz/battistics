Battistics Remote shows the battery of each Mac on your Apple Account: charge and its last day, time left, power, health and temperature, the connection, the lid and the busiest apps and accessory batteries on request. It starts and stops Keep Awake, including with the lid closed, and changes Low Power Mode.

## Setting up

1. Install Battistics on the Mac.
2. In Battistics, open **iPhone** and turn on **Show this Mac in Battistics Remote**.
3. Sign in to iCloud on the iPhone and the Mac with the same Apple Account.

There is nothing to pair. The Mac and the iPhone talk through your private iCloud database, encrypted, with no server and no open port. Closed-lid sessions from the iPhone are allowed only if you turn that on in the same pane.

## On the iPhone

- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- A Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdown with a Stop button
- A Control Center and Action Button control
- Siri and Shortcuts: Keep Awake, Let Sleep, Check Mac, Mac Low Power and Display Off
- Alerts when a session ends, the battery runs low, the Mac stops answering or reaches a charge level you pick

## Without Wi‑Fi

A Mac set to join your iPhone's Personal Hotspot automatically stays reachable from a bag. Battistics Remote reminds you before you close the lid on Wi‑Fi alone.

## Removing a Mac

If a Mac you erased or sold still shows, open its **Details** and choose **Remove Mac**. A Mac that is still sharing comes back with its next update.
