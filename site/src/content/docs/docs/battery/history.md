---
title: History
description: Charts of charge, power, temperature and health, and how the data is kept.
---

**History** charts charge, power, temperature and health by day, week, month and year, and adds up the time on battery, charging and fully charged.

## What is recorded

- A charge sample whenever the level or power source changes, and every ten minutes otherwise.
- Power samples every 60 seconds by default, while **Record power and temperature** is on in **Settings › Data**.

## How long it is kept

Charge history is kept for 400 days. Power samples are kept as recorded for 30 days and as hourly averages after that.

## Export and import

**Settings › Data** exports the full history to CSV and imports a CSV back, for a new Mac or a fresh install. The history is a database in your user folder and never leaves the Mac.
