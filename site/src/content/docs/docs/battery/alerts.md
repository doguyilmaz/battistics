---
title: Notifications
description: Battery alerts and their defaults.
---

Turn each one on or off in **Settings › Notifications**.

| Alert | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Low battery | On, at 20% |
| Further drops | On, every 5% below the low level |
| Full charge | On |
| Charge limit reached | Off, at 80% when on |
| High temperature | On, at 40 °C |
| Still on battery | Off, after 3 hours when on |
| Health decline | On |

Each alert is sent once per crossing and is remembered across a relaunch, so it does not repeat when Battistics restarts.
