---
title: Privacy
description: What Battistics reads, stores and sends.
---

- Battistics runs on your Mac. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking.
- Apart from iPhone sharing, the only network request is the optional update check.
- Battery history stays in a database in your user folder.
- iPhone sharing is off by default. When you turn it on, the Mac's status goes through your private iCloud database, encrypted, and is deleted when you turn sharing off.
- Alerts the Mac pushes to the iPhone hold one short sentence, without the Mac's name or network, since push text cannot be encrypted. They are deleted after 10 minutes.
- **Report a Problem** shows exactly what it adds to the report before you send it.
- The help assistant on macOS 26 runs on the device and only picks one of the built-in articles.

The full [privacy policy](https://www.doguyilmaz.com/legal/privacy/battistics) covers both apps.
